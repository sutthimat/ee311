Force Between Current Elements

A current element I1dL1 sets up a field dB2 at a second element, and that field pushes on it: d(dF2) = I2dL2 × dB2, which is (13). The forces on two lone elements are not equal and opposite; for complete circuits they are, as (14) shows. Two long parallel wires attract when their currents are in the same direction and repel when they are opposite, with μ0I1I2/(2πd) newtons per metre (Hayt Section 8.3).
