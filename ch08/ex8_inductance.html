Inductance and Mutual Inductance

A current I in a circuit of N turns sets up a magnetic flux, and each turn links part of it. The flux linkage per ampere is the inductance, L = NΦ/I (henrys). In a linear medium Φ grows in proportion to I, so L depends only on the shape and size of the circuit and on μ. The same L follows from the stored energy, L = 2WH/I2. The flux of one circuit that links a second circuit, per ampere, is their mutual inductance, and M12 = M21 (Hayt Section 8.10).
