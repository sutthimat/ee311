Force and Torque on a Closed Circuit

In a uniform magnetic field the forces IL × B on the sides of a closed circuit add to zero, because ∮dL = 0, but they generally form a couple: the circuit feels a torque. For a planar loop of any shape the torque is T = IS × B = m × B (18), the same about every origin, and it turns the loop until its own moment m lines up with B (Hayt Section 8.4).
