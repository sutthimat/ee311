Magnetic Boundary Conditions

At the boundary between two magnetic media the normal component of B is continuous, BN2 = BN1 (a short gaussian cylinder, ∮B · dS = 0), and the tangential component of H jumps by the free surface current, Ht1 − Ht2 = K (a small closed path, ∮H · dL = I). Normal H and tangential B change by the ratio of the permeabilities, so the flux lines bend at the boundary; a line leaving iron into air leaves almost normal to the surface (Hayt Section 8.7).
