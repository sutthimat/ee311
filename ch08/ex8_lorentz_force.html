The Lorentz Force and the Force on a Current Element

An electric field pushes any charge, F = QE; a magnetic field pushes only a moving charge, F = Qv × B, at right angles to its motion, so it bends the path but never changes the speed. Together they give the Lorentz force F = Q(E + v × B). In a conductor the force on the moving carriers passes to the conductor itself: dF = I dL × B, and F = BIL sin θ for a straight wire in a uniform field (Hayt Sections 8.1–8.2).
