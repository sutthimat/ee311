The Scalar and Vector Magnetic Potentials

Where no current flows, H = −∇Vm, and the scalar magnetic potential Vm (in amperes) obeys Laplace’s equation; but it is not single-valued: every lap round a current changes it by I, until a barrier forbids the paths that cross it. The vector magnetic potential A (in Wb/m) works everywhere: B = ∇ × A, and A is the sum of μ0I dL/(4πR) over the circuit, parallel to the current. From the definition of A, the Biot-Savart law and ∇ × H = J follow (Hayt Sections 7.6 and 7.7). Pick a mode: Explore, a slide’s example step by step, or a random drill.
