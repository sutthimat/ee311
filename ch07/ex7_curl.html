Curl

The closed line integral of H about a small path, divided by the area it encloses, tends to a limit as the path shrinks: one component of the curl, the component normal to the path. For H it is the current density, ∇ × H = J, the point form of Ampère’s circuital law; for a static E it is zero. A small paddle wheel measures it: it turns where the curl is not zero (Hayt Section 7.3). Pick a mode: Explore, the slides’ steps, Drills D7.4 and D7.5, or a random drill.
