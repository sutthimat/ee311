Stokes’ Theorem

Cut any open surface S into small elements ΔS. The closed line integral of H about each element is (∇ × H) · ΔS; added over the whole surface, every interior side is covered once in each direction and cancels, and only the outer boundary is left: ∮H · dL ≡ ∫S(∇ × H) · dS. Any surface with the same boundary gives the same value, and a closed surface gives zero, so ∇ · ∇ × A ≡ 0 (Hayt Section 7.4).
