Current Sheets, Solenoids and Toroids

Ampère’s circuital law gives H at once where the current has enough symmetry. An infinite current sheet K has H = ½K × aN on both sides, the same at every distance; two sheets ±K give K × aN between them and zero outside. Rolled into a cylinder, the sheet is a solenoid: H = (NI/d) az well inside, zero outside. Bent round into a ring, it is a toroid: H = (NI/2πρ) aφ in the core only (Hayt Section 7.2).
