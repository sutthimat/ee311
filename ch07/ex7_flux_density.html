Magnetic Flux and Flux Density

In free space the magnetic flux density is B = μ0H (teslas, Wb/m²), and the flux through a surface is Φ = ∫S B · dS (webers). Electric flux begins and ends on charges, but no source of magnetic flux has been found: every B line closes on itself, so the flux out of any closed surface is zero, ∮B · dS = 0, and ∇ · B = 0 (Hayt Section 7.5).
