Charge in a Volume

Many closely spaced charges are replaced by a smooth volume charge density ρv (C/m³): a small volume Δv holds ΔQ = ρv Δv, and the total charge is the volume integral Q = ∫vol ρv dv (Hayt §2.3). In a cylinder the element is dv = ρ dρ dφ dz; when ρv is a product of one function of each coordinate, the triple integral is a product of three single integrals.
