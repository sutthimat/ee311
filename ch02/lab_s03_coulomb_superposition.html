Superposition of point-charge fields

Notice: the total E at the probe is the vector sum of each charge's Coulomb field; field lines leave + charges and end on − charges.

Drag charges or the ◆ probe. Click empty space to add a charge (max 4). Click a charge to select it.
\mathbf E(\mathbf r)=\sum_{k}\frac{Q_k}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\,\frac{\mathbf r-\mathbf r_k}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_k|^3} Thin red/blue arrows at the probe: each charge's own contribution. Grid arrow lengths are log-scaled; the readout is exact. Charges in nC, positions in m; field shown in the plane z = 0.
