Finite vs infinite sheet of charge

Notice: close to the sheet (z ≪ a) the field is the constant ρS/2ε₀, independent of z; far away (z ≫ a) it falls as a point charge's 1/z².

E_z=\frac{\rho_S}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\iint_{\text{sheet}}\frac{z\,dx'\,dy'}{(x'^2+y'^2+z^2)^{3/2}} z\ll a:\ \ E_z\to\frac{\rho_S}{2\epsilon_0}z\gg a:\ \ E_z\to\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0 z^2},\ \ Q=\rho_S a^2 Finite sheet integrated numerically (y′ analytically, x′ by adaptive Simpson).
