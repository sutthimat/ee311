Streamlines

A streamline (flux line, direction line) is a curve that is tangent to E at every point; a free positive test charge starts to move along the streamline through its position. When E has no z component and does not change with z, the streamlines lie in planes z = constant and their slope is dy/dx = Ey/Ex (Hayt Section 2.6). The figure is a plan view of the xy-plane. In Explore, drag the point P or click anywhere: the streamline through P is drawn, with E at P and its components.

The slope equation is separated (all y on one side, all x on the other) and integrated; the constant of integration C picks one streamline, and the point P fixes it. A two-dimensional sketch is enough because E has no z component and does not depend on z: the z coordinate of P plays no part.
