A Sheet as Line-Charge Strips

Cut an infinite sheet of uniform surface charge ρS (C/m²) in the yz-plane into strips of width dy′. Each strip is an infinite line charge ρL = ρS dy′, whose field at P(x, 0, 0) is ρL/(2πε0R) along R. The strips at +y′ and −y′ form a pair: their y parts cancel and their x parts add, so only Ex is left. Summing all pairs gives Ex = ρS/(2ε0), the same at every distance x (Hayt §2.5). Pick a mode: Explore, the slide's derivation step by step, moving P, or a random drill.
