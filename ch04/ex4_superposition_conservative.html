Potential of Several Charges; Conservative Field

Potential is a scalar, so the potentials of several charges simply add: V(r) = Σ Qm/(4πε0|r − rm|) (Hayt Section 4.5, Eq. (16)); for a continuous charge the sum becomes an integral over the line, surface or volume, Eqs. (17)–(19). Because a static field has a potential, no work is done in carrying a charge around any closed path, ∮ E·dL = 0 (Eq. (20)): Kirchhoff's voltage law in its general form.
