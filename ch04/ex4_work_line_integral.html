Work and the Line Integral

To carry a charge Q against a field E, an external source does the work W = −Q∫BA E·dL from the initial point B to the final point A (Hayt Sections 4.1 and 4.2). Cut the path into small segments ΔL, multiply the field component along each by its length and add: W ≈ −Q Σ E·ΔL. W > 0: the external source does the work; W < 0: the field does the work. In a static field the answer does not depend on the path. Pick a mode: Explore, Drill D4.1, Examples 4.1 and 4.2, the line charge, Drills D4.2 and D4.3, or a random drill.

The sign of W is the classic trap. W is the work done by the external source that carries the charge. W < 0 means the charge moves with the field: the field does the work, and the external source receives energy. Keep dL in its plain form (dx ax, dρ aρ, …) and let the limits of the integral give the direction.
