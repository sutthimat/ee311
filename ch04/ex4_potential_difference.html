Potential Difference and Potential

The potential difference VAB = −∫BA E·dL is the work done by an external source in carrying a unit positive charge from B, the initial (last-named) point, to A, the final (first-named) point (Hayt Section 4.3). For a point charge Q at the origin it is VAB = (Q/4πε0)(1/rA − 1/rB). With a zero reference agreed, each point has an absolute potential, V = Q/(4πε0r) + C1, and VAB = VA − VB (Section 4.4).
