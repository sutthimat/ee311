Energy Density in the Electrostatic Field

The potential energy of a system of charges is the work an external source does to bring the charges in from infinity one by one (Hayt Section 4.8). The first charge comes into empty space and costs nothing; each later charge Qk costs Qk times the potential that the charges already in place make at its position. The total is WE = ½ Σ QmVm, or, for a continuous distribution, WE = ½ ∫ D·E dv: the energy can be thought of as stored in the field, with density ½ D·E in J/m³.
