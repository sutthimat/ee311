Spherical Differential Volume

Increasing r, θ, φ by small amounts gives a small curved box with sides dr, r dθ and r sinθ dφ. Change the values and compare the volume from the formula with the exact volume. The smaller the element, the closer the two values.

dLr = dr
dLθ = r dθ
dLφ = r sinθ dφ
dv = r² sinθ dr dθ dφ    dSr = r² sinθ dθ dφ    dSθ = r sinθ dr dφ    dSφ = r dr dθ
