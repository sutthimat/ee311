Cylindrical Conversions

A point converts with x = ρ cos φ, y = ρ sin φ, and back with ρ = √(x² + y²) and φ = tan⁻¹(y/x). A calculator's tan⁻¹ only returns angles between −90° and 90°, so the quadrant of φ is chosen from the signs of x and y. A vector at a point converts with dot products, Aρ = A·aρ and Aφ = A·aφ, and the unit vectors aρ, aφ turn with φ (Hayt Section 1.8). Drag the ringed point through all four quadrants, or pick a slide example.
