Cylindrical Coordinates at P

A point P in cylindrical coordinates is fixed by ρ, φ and z. It is the point where three coordinate surfaces meet: the cylinder ρ = constant, the half-plane φ = constant and the plane z = constant. Drag the figure to turn it, and change the values to see how the unit vectors aρ, aφ, az change direction from point to point.

Coordinate relations

x = ρ cos φ
y = ρ sin φ
z = z
ρ = √(x² + y²)    φ = tan⁻¹(y/x)    z = z    with ρ ≥ 0, 0 ≤ φ < 360°, −∞ < z < ∞
