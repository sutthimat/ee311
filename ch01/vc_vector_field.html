Vector Fields

A vector field assigns a vector, with a magnitude and a direction, to every point in space; a scalar field assigns only a number. Each grey arrow is the field at the grid point where its tail sits. Drag the figure to turn it, and drag the orange point P (or set its coordinates) to read the field there.

The field at P

In rectangular components a vector field is F(x, y, z) = Fx ax + Fy ay + Fz az, where each component is itself a scalar field. Its magnitude is |F| = √(Fx² + Fy² + Fz²) and its direction is the unit vector aF = F/|F|. The angle θ is measured from the +z axis and φ from the +x axis in the xy-plane. All values are given to 3 significant figures.
