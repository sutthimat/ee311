The Gradient

The gradient ∇f of a scalar field f(x, y) is a vector. It points in the direction in which f increases fastest, its magnitude |∇f| is that largest rate of increase, and it is perpendicular to the level curve through the point. The rate of increase in any other direction au is the directional derivative Duf = ∇f · au. For a potential, Hayt writes E = −∇V: the electric field points the way the potential falls fastest.
