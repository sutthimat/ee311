Boundary Conditions for Perfect Dielectrics

At the boundary between two perfect dielectrics with no free surface charge, the tangential component of E and the normal component of D are continuous: Etan1 = Etan2 (small closed path) and DN1 = DN2 (small gaussian pillbox). Tangential D and normal E change by the ratio of the permittivities, so the flux lines bend at the boundary: tan θ1/tan θ2 = ε1/ε2 (Hayt Section 5.8).
