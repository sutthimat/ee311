Conductor Boundary Conditions

In the static case the field inside a conductor is zero and so is the volume charge density; any charge sits on the surface as ρS. Just outside, the tangential field is zero and the normal flux density equals the surface charge: Dt = Et = 0 and DN = ε0EN = ρS (Hayt Section 5.4). The small closed path abcda gives the first result, the small gaussian cylinder (pillbox) the second. Pick a mode: Explore, Examples 5.2 and 5.3 and Drill D5.5 step by step, or a random drill.
