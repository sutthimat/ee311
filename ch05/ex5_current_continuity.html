Current Density and Continuity

Charge in motion is a current, I = dQ/dt, and the current density J (A/m²) gives the current through any surface, I = ∫S J·dS (Hayt Section 5.1). A charge cloud of density ρv moving with velocity v carries the convection current density J = ρv v. Charge is conserved: the current leaving a closed surface is the rate of decrease of the charge inside, ∮S J·dS = −dQi/dt, or ∇·J = −∂ρv/∂t at a point (Section 5.2).
