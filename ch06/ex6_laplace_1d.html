Solving Laplace’s Equation in One Dimension

When the potential depends on one coordinate only, Laplace’s equation ∇²V = 0 can be integrated twice: the general solution has two constants A and B, and the two boundary conditions (the potentials of the two conductors) fix them. By the uniqueness theorem that solution is the only one. From V follow E = −∇V, D = εE, the surface charge ρS = DN on a conductor, its charge Q and the capacitance C = Q/V0 (Hayt Section 6.7). Pick one of the five problems, or step through Examples 6.2–6.6, Drill D6.6 or a random drill.
