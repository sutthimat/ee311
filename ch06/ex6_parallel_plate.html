Capacitance and the Parallel-Plate Capacitor

Two conductors carry equal and opposite charges +Q and −Q. The flux leaves the positive conductor and ends on the negative one, so the positive conductor is at the higher potential, and V0 is the potential difference. The capacitance is C = Q/V0. Multiply the charge by N and D, E and V0 are all multiplied by N, so C depends only on the geometry and the permittivity (Hayt Section 6.1). For two parallel plates of area S a distance d apart, C = εS/d, and the stored energy is WE = ½CV0² (Section 6.2).
