Several Capacitance Examples

The capacitance C = Q/V0 of two conductors depends only on their shape, their spacing and the dielectric between them. Assume a charge Q, find D by Gauss’s law and E = D/ε, integrate E from one conductor to the other for V0, and divide. With two dielectrics, DN is continuous across layers parallel to the plates (capacitors in series) and Et is continuous across an interface normal to them (capacitors in parallel) (Hayt Section 6.3).
