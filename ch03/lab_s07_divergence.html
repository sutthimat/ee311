Divergence = outward flux per unit volume

Notice: as the box shrinks, ∮D·dS / Δv → ∇·D at its centre. Flowing ≠ diverging: y ax and 1/r² (away from the source) have ∇·D = 0.

\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\lim_{\Delta v\to0}\frac{\oint_S\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf S}{\Delta v} Point sources (dots) make ∮ = ±4π if inside, so the ratio blows up. Box: cube of side Δx (2-D view of the z = 0 slice). Flux computed by 2-D Gauss–Legendre quadrature over all six faces. Drag the box.
