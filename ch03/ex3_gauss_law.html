Gauss's Law on Any Closed Surface

The electric flux leaving any closed surface equals the charge it encloses: Ψ = ∮S DS·dS = Q (Hayt §3.2). A patch ΔS of the surface carries the flux ΔΨ = DS cos θ ΔS = DS·ΔS, where θ is the angle between DS and the outward normal. Reshape the surface or move the charges: Ψ changes only when a charge crosses the surface. Pick a mode: Explore, Example 3.1 and Drills D3.3, D3.4 step by step, or a random drill.
