Gauss's Law with Symmetry

Gauss's law, Ψ = ∮ DS·dS = Q, holds for every closed surface, but it gives D only on a surface where DS is everywhere normal or tangential to the surface and DS is constant where it is normal (Hayt Section 3.3). Symmetry picks that surface: a sphere for a point charge, a cylinder for a line charge or a coaxial cable. Choose a charge and a gaussian surface, or step through the slides' examples and drill.
