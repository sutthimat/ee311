Electric Flux and Flux Density

In Faraday's experiment a charge +Q on an inner metal sphere induces −Q on an outer sphere around it, whatever the dielectric between them: something passes from one to the other, the electric flux Ψ = Q. Spread over a sphere of radius r it gives the electric flux density D = Q/(4πr²) ar (C/m²), which has no ε0 in it; in free space D = ε0E (Hayt Section 3.1).
