The Divergence Theorem

The flux leaving a closed surface equals the volume integral of the divergence inside it: ∮S D·dS = ∫vol ∇·D dv (Hayt §3.6). Split the volume into small cells. The outflow of each cell is (∇·D) Δv; a face shared by two cells carries flux out of one and into the other, so in the sum over all cells it counts twice with opposite signs and cancels. Only the outer surface is left. Pick a mode: Explore, Example 3.5 and Drill D3.9 step by step, or a random drill.
