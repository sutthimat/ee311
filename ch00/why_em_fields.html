Why Electromagnetic Fields

Electromagnetic Fields · Introduction · Topic 1 · Slide 1

What Can This Course Explain?

Three components every student has met in circuit theory. Circuit theory tells us how they behave through C, L and M; it does not tell us why. Operate each one and ask the question beneath it.

(a) Energy stored in a capacitor

The battery has been removed, yet the lamp still lights. Where was the energy stored?

(b) Force in a solenoid actuator

Nothing touches the plunger. Why is it pulled in, why does the force grow as I², and why does it stay in at a much lower current?

(c) Induction between separate coils

The coils share no wire. Why does a voltage appear across coil 2, and why does a steady DC current produce none?
